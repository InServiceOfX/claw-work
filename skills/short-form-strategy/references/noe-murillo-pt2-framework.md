# Noe Murillo — Part 2 (tips, money, what to avoid)

Parsed 2026-09-23 from `Data/Public/tiktoks/How to be a Successful Content Creator pt 2.mp4`. Timestamped speech is `pt2.srt`. Frames are matched in `Data/Public/tiktoks/SCREEN-MATCH.md`.

Part 1 is how a video gets watched. Part 2 is what to do so one viral video is not the whole business, and so the next video is still his.

Every dollar figure below is his claim, including the pie chart on screen at about 0:30. Do not repeat those numbers as verified income.

## Rules

1. **Cross-post the same short.** He names Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube. He says Facebook is where his videos travel farthest right now. Do not depend on one app.
2. **Views are not the business.** He says the algorithm is uneven and that brand deals and partnerships are most of the money. A music mix has no brand-deal path in this video. Use the rule as: do not expect the 21-minute upload to distribute itself.
3. **Widen the niche without leaving it.** Food reviews plus cooking plus a cleaning video, still one kitchen. For a mix: the long video is the mix, the short is the argument about the mix. Do not turn the short into an AI demo unless that is the channel.
4. **Be able to talk.** Silent clips get more views, he says, and brands still ask for a spoken 30 seconds. A mix short can stay silent if the beat and the on-screen question are the voice. A one-line spoken open is the version that proves a person is behind it.
5. **People follow a person, not a format.** Day-in-the-life is his example. For this mix, the recurring bit is the question "who did it best?", not a new format every post.
6. **Do not clone a tired format.** Same sound, same hook, same style as everyone else. Pivot when the format is crowded.
7. **The hit has to be because of the creator.** A clip that only works if a stranger reacts will die when nobody reacts. Do not build the Who Shot Ya posts around hoping a famous person duets them.
8. **Study openings, edits, and what they say.** He tells viewers to watch smaller creators who still get deals, and personality-led vloggers. Copy the structure (cold open, cut, comment prompt). Do not copy their niche.

## If he were starting over

Day in the life. Food, because everyone eats. Mukbang or cooking. He would not start with solo dates or POV food reviews. He says those formats are burnt out.

## How this applies to a finished long video

The long file stays on YouTube. A vertical short carries the hook and one comment question, then points at the long URL. Cross-post the short. Reply to people who answer. Do not cold-message strangers.
