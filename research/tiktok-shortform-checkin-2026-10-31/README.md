## Themes

### 1. Script rhythm

Mix short sentences of 5 words or fewer between long ones (long, short, long,
short). The short lines carry story turns ("Let's find out.", "But there was a
problem."). Credited to @kallaway. Now a `claw-shorts check` warning.

### 2. Voice audio without a better mic

EQ (vocal-enhancer preset, a little more high end), a hard limiter at -2 dB with
input boost, then loudness to -14 LUFS integrated / -1 dBTP. Measure instead of
trusting headphones. Now applied to every `claw-shorts` and `shortform-video` render.

### 3. What AI does well in editing

After a month of tests: AI is strong at motion design, rough cuts and captions,
and weak at scripts (cheesy, repetitive as length grows). He writes scripts
himself and batches 3-5 projects for AI processing.
