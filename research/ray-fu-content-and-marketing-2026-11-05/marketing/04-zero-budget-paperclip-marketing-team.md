## Marketing org (as described)
- Content Strategist — trends via “Verlo” (spoken; treat as trend source) + calendar
- Video Editor — Remotion skill for UGC/shortform
- Social Media Manager — schedule TikTok/IG/LinkedIn/X
- QA — review before publish

## Routines
- **Daily:** strategist viral check → brief → editor + social → QA → draft schedule
- **Weekly:** strategist reviews performance → next week plan
- Ship hooks: connect product link, GitHub repo, Discord/Slack/newsletter; on feature push, agent drafts multi-channel update

## Zero-paid-SaaS
Paperclip is OSS/self-hosted — fits constraints. Pair with Remotion + Claude/Codex/Grok adapters. Replace any paid trend SaaS with Agent-Reach / YouTube/Reddit sweeps.
