## 7 agents (chain with analytics → strategy feedback)
1. Content Strategy — trends, competitors, calendar
2. Script Writer — hook/story/CTA templates
3. Thumbnail Designer — A/B variants (shock / curiosity+payoff / insight-led — shown on-screen)
4. SEO Optimizer — title, description, tags
5. Production Management — TTS + assets + assemble MP4
6. Publishing & Scheduling — upload/schedule (keep human approve)
7. Analytics — performance → next topics
