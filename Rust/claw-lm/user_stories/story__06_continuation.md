# 06 — Continue across sessions

As a user, I want current Markdown handoff notes so another session or AI harness
can continue after an interruption or usage limit.

Status: implemented; scoped evidence and limits in [VERIFICATION.md](../docs/VERIFICATION.md).

- A newcomer finds branch, approval status, source pins, completed changes, exact
  verification commands, observed results and next steps.
- Distinguish research, executable code, synthetic tests, real weights and actual
  client evidence. Never invent a successful PDD application or model run.
- Record blockers and temporary upstream checkout locations. Missing context is
  not evidence of prior approval.
- Refresh notes at milestones and before ending a session. Exclude private paths
  and raw conversations from public notes and preserve previous agents' work.

Evidence: follow `docs/HANDOFF.md` from a fresh session and verify its claims,
links, commands and branch state.
