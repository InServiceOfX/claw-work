My captions were hiding under TikTok's buttons. I never noticed. A free tool did.

The fix is HyperFrames. You write the video in HTML, and it renders on your own machine.

It checks every frame against TikTok, Reels and Shorts. That's how it caught mine.

Now the receipts. It timed every word of a 25-second voiceover in 2.5 seconds. A 27-second short rendered in under 14 seconds.

It's free, Apache 2, and local. One catch: turn telemetry off.
